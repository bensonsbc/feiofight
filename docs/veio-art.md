# Véio — direção de arte

Os dois PNGs foram gerados com a ferramenta integrada de imagens. A fotografia fornecida foi a referência de identidade; `bale-sheet.png` foi usada somente como referência de estilo e estrutura da folha.

## Retrato — prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: main fighter portrait for the existing arcade fighting game “Luta de Feio”. Create a single full-body fighter portrait from the identity photo, preserving the mature man's recognizable face, bald head, full gray beard and mustache and robust/medium build. Remove the eyeglasses hanging from his shirt. Dress him in a washed charcoal-gray V-neck T-shirt, dark pants and discreet dark footwear. Show him centered, head to shoes, in a grounded right-facing fighting stance on a solid deep navy background. Use polished, authentic early-1990s 16-bit arcade pixel art with crisp deliberate pixels, readable silhouette and limited shading. Exactly one human; no text, logos, interface, props, watermark or cropping.

## Folha de sprites — prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production sprite sheet for “Luta de Feio”. Create a finished 1024 × 1536 sheet titled exactly “VÉIO”, following the existing sheet's seven-row organization, label placement, four-frame rhythm, scale and authentic 1990s 16-bit arcade style. Preserve the identity and outfit in every frame, always facing right, on a flat medium-light gray background. Add exactly seven rows labeled “ANDAR”, “PULAR”, “ABAIXAR”, “CHUTE”, “SOCO”, “ESPECIAL”, “DEFESA”, with exactly four human figures per row and 28 total. The Arroto Radioativo sequence shows Véio holding his stomach, opening his mouth as green vapor begins, emitting a bright toxic-green gaseous cloud shaped like a skull and recovering while that cloud travels farther right. Keep the cloud visibly detached and crop-ready. The skull exists only as luminous gas, with no solid skull or gore. No extra people, props, scenery, logos, interface, watermark or extra text.

## Correção e integração

A primeira folha deixou a nuvem do quarto quadro especial cortada pela borda. Foi aplicada uma correção dirigida apenas para mover e reduzir essa nuvem, mantendo-a inteira dentro da tela. O renderizador recorta o efeito completo da terceira célula, onde a caveira tem maior leitura, remove o fundo cinza e aplica brilho verde durante o voo. A animação do personagem mantém o vapor saindo da boca antes do projétil.
