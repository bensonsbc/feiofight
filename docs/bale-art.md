# Bale — direção de arte

Os dois PNGs foram gerados com a ferramenta integrada de imagens. A fotografia fornecida foi a referência de identidade; `ratao-sheet.png` foi usada somente como referência de estilo e estrutura da folha.

## Retrato — prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: game fighter character portrait. Create one full-body 16-bit early-1990s arcade fighter portrait of BALE, recognizable as the man in the identity photo. Show his notably short adult stature respectfully through compact proportions, preserving his oval face, short graying brown hair, thin rectangular glasses and friendly expression. Adapt him into a right-facing fighting stance with a maroon logo-free athletic T-shirt, dark navy knee-length shorts and dark sneakers. Use crisp hard-edged pixels, a limited palette, chunky dark outlines and selective dithering. Center exactly one complete human on a plain deep-navy background. No props, text, logos, watermark, caricature or mockery.

## Folha de sprites — prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 16-bit arcade fighting-game sprite sheet. Create a 1024 × 1536 sheet titled “BALE”, matching the existing grid, labels and rendering style. Bale must remain visibly 20–25% shorter than the reference fighter scale, with consistent identity and outfit, facing right. Add seven rows labeled exactly “ANDAR”, “PULAR”, “ABAIXAR”, “CHUTE”, “SOCO”, “ESPECIAL”, “DEFESA”, with four frames per row. The Turma dos Sete special shows Bale calling seven tiny, wholly original public-domain fairy-tale miner warriors who rush right as a group. Give them diverse caps, clothes, faces, proportions and tools. Keep the final group detached and crop-able. Do not depict Snow White or copy Disney designs, names, costumes, trademarks or visual styling. Use a flat light-gray background; no extra labels, logos or watermark.

## Integração

A geração não colocou Bale em duas células intermediárias do especial e reuniu seis mineiros completos na última célula. O renderizador usa as poses de chamada e recuperação de Bale e combina o grupo final com um mineiro original de outra célula, formando sete atacantes visíveis no jogo sem alterar os demais quadros.
